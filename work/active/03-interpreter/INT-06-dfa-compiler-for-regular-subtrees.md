# INT-06: DFA compiler for regular subtrees

## Summary

Regular character subtrees and `Longest` over regular alternatives compile to DFA tables that agree with the interpreter's commit semantics ([ADR-0017](../../docs/decisions/0017-regular-subtrees-compile-to-an-eager-commit-aware-automaton.md)). The same automata library serves analysis (CRL0101, CRL0103, CRL0106) and fusion.

## Scope

- **The Regular predicate.** It goes in `src/Creole/Analysis/` (ADR-0017 point 1), and ANA-01 reuses it.
  - Character input only.
  - The node kinds the prototypes tested: character classes, `String`, `Sequence`, `Or`, `Optional`, `Repeat` and `SkipRepeat` with a minimum of 0 or 1 and no maximum, and `Atomic`. A `Longest` compiles when its alternatives are all Regular; a `Longest` written directly as an alternative of another joins it, and one nested anywhere else is not Regular.
  - The value-only wrappers `Map`, `Slice`, `Text`, `Named` and `Context`.
  - Every choice inside an explicit `Atomic` meets fragment E′'s condition ([prototype note](../../docs/research/2026-10-23-commit-aware-automaton-prototypes.md), finding 19).
  - Another node kind joins only with differential tests that cover it.
- **The eager commit-aware automaton** (prototype note, findings 13–16).
  - Construction: thread lists determinized into states, over character classes from a sweep of `CharSet` boundaries.
  - Minimization only if it pays.
  - `Longest` becomes one automaton with a register per alternative, and ties go to the earliest.
- **Lowering.**
  - Tables are built in full at lowering, so plans stay immutable.
  - Above a state cap, the subtree runs on the interpreter.
  - Lowering time is measured on the JSON and C#-like lexers. If tuned construction is still slow, lazy construction needs its own decision (ADR-0017 point 3).
- **Execution.**
  - The plan runs maximal munch with backup, as a lowering rewrite under `LoweringOptions`.
  - It is kept or dropped by benchmark, as INT-05's rewrites were. Which non-`Longest` subtrees are worth compiling is set by measurement.
- **Values and failures** (ADR-0017 points 4–6).
  - A value that is a function of the span comes from the span. Otherwise the winning alternative is re-run.
  - When the automaton finds no match, the interpreter re-runs the subtree.
  - How far each scan read is exposed by ERR-02, in the form its choice of mechanism needs (moved there with the maintainer's approval).
- **Not compiled.**
  - `Lookahead`, `Not`, `Cut`.
  - Token-level input.
  - Choices inside `Atomic` outside E′.
  - The deferred automaton and Option B's condition are not built (ADR-0017 point 8).
- **Docs.** `docs/engines/interpreter.md` gains a section on which subtrees compile and how to write them so they do.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Compiled plans agree with the interpreter with the rewrite on and off (`Rewrites.Compare`) on property-generated terms and inputs. The comparison covers outcome, consumed length, commit flag, winning alternative, value and cut flag. The generated terms include nested `Atomic`, `String` literals, `Longest` and depth 5.
- [x] The Regular predicate gives the expected answer on a table of known terms. The table includes both prototype lexers and the counterexamples of the prototype note's findings 2 and 19.
- [x] Subtrees over the state cap or outside E′ run on the interpreter, and the differential tests cover them.
- [x] Lowering time and tokenizing speed for the JSON and C#-like lexers are measured, median of three runs, and recorded on this card. The rewrite is kept only if it beats the interpreter.

- 2026-10-23 (Research): Started. Build is premature: what the DFA must compute is not settled. Commit semantics are not regex semantics, even under `Longest`. `OptionalPlan` fails committed when its item fails after consuming, and repetition is greedy, so on `1.x` the alternative `Seq(Digits, Optional(Seq('.', Digits)))` fails, while a maximal-munch DFA over its regex reading returns `1`. Code: scratchpad only, not kept. ## Outcome

A `Longest` over characters whose alternatives are all Regular now runs as one eager commit-aware automaton under `LoweringOptions.Dfa`, and gives exactly the interpreter's results. Tokenizing 100 KB was 3.31× faster with a 12-rule JSON lexer and 13.58× faster with a 138-rule C#-like lexer, medians of three runs, so the rewrite is kept. Anything else still runs on the interpreter.

- **Regular predicate:** `src/Creole/Analysis/Regular.cs` and `RegularTerm.cs` (`Regular.Of`), which ANA-01 is to reuse.
- **Automaton:** `src/Creole/Analysis/CommitAutomaton.cs`. Construction was tuned from 769 ms to about 107–161 ms for the C#-like lexer. It has 39 states for JSON and 513 for C#, and `PlanBuilder.DfaStateCap` is 2,000.
- **Plan and lowering:** `src/Creole/Plans/DfaLongestPlan.cs`, `PlanBuilder.Dfa.cs`, `MatchValue.cs`; `Parser.ValueOfMatch()` with overrides on the value-only and leaf nodes; `MapNode.IgnoresSource`, which `As` and `Ignore` set; `Cursor.ItemAt`. A `Longest` written directly as an alternative of another is flattened into it.
- **Bug fixed along the way:** `Cursor.Reset` threw when moving to the end of segmented input from an earlier segment, which the interpreter's `LongestPlan` hit.
- **Tests:** `RegularTests` (50), `CommitAutomatonTests` (9), `DfaLoweringTests` (12), 2 new `AllocationTests` cases and 3 new `CursorTests`, with shared `RegularShapes` and `LexerSamples`.
- **Benchmarks:** `LoweringBenchmarks` gains `DfaJsonLexer`, `DfaCSharpLexer`, `DfaLowerJson`, `DfaLowerCSharp` and the `DfaCharTokens` trial. The lexers are in `LongestLexers`, and the C#-like input is `LoweringInputs.CSharp` (hash pinned).
- **Docs:** the "When a Longest compiles" section of [docs/engines/interpreter.md](../../../docs/engines/interpreter.md#when-a-longest-compiles), [docs/concepts/choice.md](../../../docs/concepts/choice.md), and [benchmarks/README.md](../../../benchmarks/README.md).

The whitepaper's § Properties, § Longest match, § Interpreted engine and § Bookmarks and retention reflect it.

  - The three measured runs used a build that passed the fallback plan as a constructor parameter, since folded back into a field with identical behaviour.
