# Editing research notes

Full rules: CONTRIBUTING.md § Research notes. Template: work/docs/research/README.md.

- Cite only sources actually read in this session or already in the whitepaper's References. Record how much of each was read. Mark anything from memory *unverified*.
- Keep what a source claims, what was measured, and what we infer visibly separate.
- Every note has a "Disconfirming evidence" section that says what was searched, even when nothing was found.
- A finished note is a dated record. Do not rewrite its conclusions later; write a new note and archive the old one with a pointer to its replacement.
- Research notes never change the whitepaper. Decisions do.
- Add each new note to the index table in work/docs/research/README.md and to the `## Artifacts` of the cards it serves.
